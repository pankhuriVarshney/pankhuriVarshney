**MAYA** is an autonomous deception technology platform built to make attackers reveal their TTPs before they reach real infrastructure.

- 🎯 **Fake asset orchestration** — Dynamic decoy services, credentials, and endpoints that look indistinguishable from production
- 🔍 **Lateral movement detection** — Catches attackers pivoting through the environment in real time
- 🧬 **Adversary profiling** — Builds a behavioral fingerprint of every attacker who interacts with the system
- 📡 **SIEM pipeline integration** — Feeds structured threat intel directly into Splunk/ELK
- 🤖 **State-synchronized** — Decoys adapt dynamically based on attacker behavior

> *Think of it as giving your SOC a window into the attacker's mind — before they've touched anything real.*
